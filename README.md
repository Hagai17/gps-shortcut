Entry for the make.fun bounty [The GPS Swears It Was a Shortcut](https://make.fun/c/u-alven-the-gps-swears-it-was-a-shortcut-2cac21d9?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=gps-shortcut).

`main.py` writes the GPS's one-line excuse to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The map called this a drive-through attraction, and a drive-through is a shortcut by definition. The lions are fed at four. It is 3:58. Please do not be the slowest vehicle here.

The line was picked by duelling twelve drafts against the competition's
published judge brief and the live field's strongest lines in both orders
(full drafts, field scan and duel record in
[candidates.md](candidates.md)). The field stampeded into the judge
brief's own example details — of 1,183 unique finished lines, 485 use a
lake, 302 a wedding, 177 a cul-de-sac and 121 open on "recalculating" —
so this entry pleads its case in an empty lane instead: it argues the
excuse out loud ("a shortcut by definition"), blames the map's label,
and closes on a feeding-time clock in the GPS's polite instruction
voice.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt and prints the line
```
